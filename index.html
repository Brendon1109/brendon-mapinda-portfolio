FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Brendon Mapinda do?

I run a Cape Town creative studio and automation lab. The Studio delivers cinematic photography and video — editorial portraits, lifestyle shoots, short-form reels and event coverage. The Systems side delivers AI automation, software and app development, and business optimization. You can hire either discipline, or both.

Where are you based and who do you work with?

I'm based in Cape Town, South Africa. Creative shoots are available city-wide; AI automation, software and business-optimization work is delivered for clients across South Africa and remotely worldwide.

How much do photography sessions cost?

Studio sessions start at R850 (Mini), R1,350 (Signature — most booked, includes a cinematic reel) and R2,000–R2,500 (Premium Creative). Event coverage starts at R1,800. Every session includes creative direction, Lightroom retouching and a private gallery.

What do the AI & software engagements cost?

Indicative starting points: an Automation Sprint from R7,500, a Custom Build from R25,000, and an ongoing Systems Retainer from R4,500/month. Every build is scoped to the problem — book a free discovery call and we'll size it properly.

What is AI discoverability — can you get my business found in ChatGPT or Gemini?

AI discoverability means structuring your site and content so AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews — can find, understand and recommend you. I offer it as a service, using structured data, clean semantic content, an llms.txt file and AI-crawler-friendly configuration. This site uses the same techniques.
